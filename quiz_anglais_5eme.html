Quiz Anglais 5ème

Grammar & Vocabulary – 15 questions

1. Choose the correct past simple: 'Yesterday, she ___ (go) to school.'
went : 'go' is an irregular verb. Past simple = went. (go → went → gone)
2. Which sentence is in the Present Perfect ?
She has been to Paris. = Present perfect : have/has + past participle. Expresses an experience or a past action with a present result.
3. 'I ___ (not/see) that film yet.' Fill in with Present Perfect.
haven't seen : present perfect negative = have/has + not + past participle. 'Yet' is a typical marker.
4. Translate: 'Il va pleuvoir demain.' (future with 'will')
It will rain tomorrow. : 'will' + infinitive = prediction about the future.
5. 'She ___ (study) when I called.' (past continuous)
was studying : past continuous = was/were + verb-ing. Action in progress interrupted by another.
6. What does 'should' express ?
Advice or recommendation : You should study more. (= I recommend it). Should = devoir (conseil).
7. Choose the right form: 'If I ___ rich, I would travel.'
were : conditional type 2. 'If' + past (were for all persons) → would + infinitive. Expresses an unreal hypothesis.
8. Passive voice: 'The teacher corrects the tests.' Passive?
The tests are corrected by the teacher. : passive voice = to be + past participle + by + agent.
9. Choose the correct word: 'It's ___ cold today !' (emphasis)
very : modifies an adjective (cold). Much = uncountable nouns. Many = countable nouns.
10. 'I have lived here ___ 5 years.' Choose the right preposition.
for : expresses a duration (pendant 5 ans). Since = depuis un moment precis (since 2019).
11. Which verb is irregular in past simple ?
Speak : irregular → spoke (past simple), spoken (past participle). The others add -ed.
12. 'She is ___ intelligent ___ her brother.' (comparaison)
more... than : comparative of superiority with long adjectives (2+ syllables): more + adjective + than.
13. 'London is ___ city in the UK.' (superlative)
the biggest : superlative = the + adjective + -est (short adjective). Big → biggest (double the g).
14. Reported speech: She said: 'I am tired.' → She said that...
she was tired. : reported speech = backshift. Present simple → past simple. 'I' → 'she'.
15. Choose the correct question tag: 'You like pizza, ___?'
don't you? : positive statement → negative tag. 'Like' is present simple, so auxiliary 'do' → 'don't you?'
