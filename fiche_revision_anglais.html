⏱️ I. Le Prétérit simple (Simple Past)

Quand l'utiliser ?

• Action terminée dans le passé, à un moment précis.

• Souvent avec : yesterday, last week, in 2020, ago, when...

Verbe régulier : infinitif + -ed · Verbe irrégulier : forme spéciale
TypeAffirmationNégationQuestion
RégulierI walkedI didn't walkDid I walk?
IrrégulierShe wentShe didn't goDid she go?
Être (be)He wasHe wasn'tWas he?

⚠️ Piège

À la négation et à la question, on utilise did/didn't + infinitif (sans -ed) !

✓ She didn't go → ✗ She didn't went

✓ Did he play? → ✗ Did he played?

🔄 II. Le Présent Perfect (Have + participe passé)

Quand l'utiliser ?

• Expérience de vie (sans préciser quand) : I have never been to New York.

• Action passée avec conséquence dans le présent : I have lost my keys (→ I can't open the door).

• Souvent avec : already, yet, never, ever, just, since, for...

Subject + have/has + past participle (pp)
PronomAffirmationNégationQuestion
I / You / We / TheyI have seenI haven't seenHave I seen?
He / She / ItShe has seenShe hasn't seenHas she seen?

⚠️ Simple Past vs Present Perfect

• Simple Past = moment précis dans le passé → I saw him yesterday.

• Present Perfect = expérience ou résultat actuel → I have seen that film.

🔮 III. Le Conditionnel (Would)

Quand l'utiliser ?

• Hypothèse : If I had more time, I would study more.

• Souhait poli : I would like a coffee, please.

• Futur dans le passé : She said she would come.

Subject + would (= 'd) + infinitif
AffirmationNégationQuestion
I would go / I'd goI wouldn't goWould I go?

⚠️ Piège conditionnel

• Type 1 : If + present simple → will + inf (futur possible) → If it rains, I will stay.

• Type 2 : If + past simple → would + inf (hypothèse peu probable) → If I won, I would buy a car.

⚠️ Règle des modaux

Après un modal, le verbe est TOUJOURS à l'infinitif sans "to" !

✓ She can swim → ✗ She can to swim / She can swims
