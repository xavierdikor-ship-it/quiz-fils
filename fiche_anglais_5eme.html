Grammar & Vocabulary – Niveau avancé 5ème

1. Les temps du passé

TempsFormationEmploiExemple
Past Simpleverb + -ed (reg.) / forme irrégulièreAction terminée dans le passéShe went to school yesterday.
Past Continuouswas/were + verb-ingAction en cours dans le passé, interrompueI was studying when she called.
Present Perfecthave/has + past participleAction passée liée au présent, expérienceI have never been to Japan.
💡 Marqueurs : yesterday/ago → past simple | just/already/yet/never/ever/since/for → present perfect
Verbes irréguliers à connaître :
go → went → gone | see → saw → seen | speak → spoke → spoken
write → wrote → written | take → took → taken | come → came → come
buy → bought → bought | know → knew → known | give → gave → given

2. Le futur

FormeEmploiExemple
will + infinitifPrédiction, décision spontanéeIt will rain tomorrow.
going to + infinitifIntention planifiée, preuve visibleShe is going to study medicine.
Present continuousPlan arrangé à l'avanceWe are meeting at 6.

3. Les modaux

ModalSensExemple
can / couldCapacité, possibilitéShe can swim fast.
must / have toObligationYou must wear a seatbelt.
shouldConseil, recommandationYou should sleep more.
might / mayPossibilité (incertaine)It might snow tonight.
mustn'tInterdictionYou mustn't run here.
don't have toPas d'obligationYou don't have to come.

4. Comparatif et Superlatif

Adjectif court (<2 syl.)Adjectif long (≥2 syl.)
Comparatifadj + -er + than (taller than)more + adj + than (more intelligent than)
Superlatifthe + adj + -est (the tallest)the most + adj (the most intelligent)
Irréguliers : good → better → the best | bad → worse → the worst | far → farther → the farthest
✅ London is bigger than Paris. / London is the biggest city in the UK.

5. La voix passive

Formation : to be (conjugué) + past participle (+ by + agent)
Active : The teacher corrects the tests.
Passive : The tests are corrected by the teacher.
TempsPassif
Present simpleis/are + past participle
Past simplewas/were + past participle
Present perfecthas/have been + past participle

6. Discours indirect (Reported Speech)

Quand on rapporte des paroles au passé, les temps reculent :
present simple → past simple
past simple → past perfect
will → would | can → could | am/is/are → was/were
She said: "I am tired." → She said that she was tired.
He said: "I will come." → He said that he would come.

7. Les conditionnels

TypeStructureEmploi
Type 1 (réel)If + present → willSi tu travailles, tu réussiras.
Type 2 (irréel présent)If + past (were) → wouldIf I were rich, I would travel.
💡 Type 2 : on utilise toujours "were" (pas "was") après "if", même avec I/he/she.

8. Question Tags

Phrase positive → tag négatif | Phrase négative → tag positif
Utilise l'auxiliaire de la phrase principale.
✅ You like pizza, don't you? | She can swim, can't she?
✅ He doesn't work here, does he? | They weren't late, were they?
